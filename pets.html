Pets, Pornography, and Property Ownership

Middle class white collar millenials in highly urbanised societies are currently excluded from the property market. The resulting "luxury poverty" has made them vulnerable to psychological warfare from the pet food industry, which has taken the opportunity to engage in cultural terrorism against human relationships.

I want to outline a process mediated by advertising, by which:

  1. people engage in 'emotional bestiality' where pets meet a social need in an inferior, self-limiting way which ought to be considered disordered, and
  2. this is encouraged because it is profitable
This leads to 'pet addiction', which is to emotional affection what porn addiction is to sex. The ultimate results are narcisistic behaviours that shame us in the eye of God

Pet Addiction

The contemporary relationship between humans and their pets (especially dogs) is the idealised form of a co-dependant relationship. A codependant (human) relationship is an unhealthy dynamic where one partner needs the other partner, who in turn, needs to be needed. People who own pets can experience 'love' without having to deal with the actual messy reality of a human-human relationship, or the need to compromise and engage with different thought or value systems. This means they can avoid confronting flaws or thorns within themselves, because the pet will love them unconditionally. This unconditional love which dogs offer their owners sounds good but is in reality a satanic[1] arrangement. In this way, when humans love pets as 'members of the family' or in lieu of a romantic partner[2], they are meeting some of the emotional needs that drive us into relationship with each other (specifically, the need to give and receive affection) with an animal instead of a human. This is the analogy to pornography: people use pornography as an accessible substitute for sex when they want to alleviate sexual desire. Pornography addiction occurs when the substitute takes over and impedes the persons ability to participate fully in normal adult sexual relationships. My hope is that in the future, the tendency for people to speak about or treat their pets as if they are people and especially members of the family will be a diagnosable mental health disorder, in acknowledgement of the inevitable harm of the substitution of real human relationships with a servile proxy.


The Role of Advertising

The unavailability of housing in desirable locations means that white collar millenials have, in the short term, much more disposable income than otherwise. If houses were more affordable, young people would have a 'sink' for basically an infinite amount of excess money. Without a mortage or a desire to save for one, more free cash is available. This clearly represents an opportunity for industries to make profits (for example, food delivery/takeaway, and ridesourcing). And Pets. Historically[3] pets had a finite value to the household: they were a valued part of the household, but not an irreplaceable part. Since then, we have been bombarded with advertising designed to convince us that our pets are in fact equivalent to human members of the household, and that our economic/material obligation to them (like to our spouse or children) is virtually limitless. The purpose of this advertising, like all advertising, is to induce us to open our wallets and purchase things for our pets ranging from medical care and insurance through to luxury pet food, clothes and toys. It is obvious that the change in our attitude to pets is the product of advertising rather than a 'natural' social shift. It is obvious because of the stark contrast to the reduction in almost all other bonds of obligation and especially economic obligation. The structural, and some say terminal decline in networks of obligation and non-transactional reciprocity have declined systematically across all areas of our lives: except where it is profitable, such as towards the animals that share our homes.

Resulting disgraceful behaviour

The primary local harm from the cuckoo-like transformation of pets into humans in order to coerce us to spend more money on them is to the pet owner. By socially sanctioning the satanic substitution of real, reciprocal, challenging human connection with on-tap, unconditional, co-dependant human-animal connection we encourage an endless wallowing and stagnation in the pet owner. But while this is embarassing and limiting for an individual, it does not bring shame before god. I think that even without a belief in god, it is good to imagine having to 'meet your maker' at the end of your life. The experience of coming face-to-face with a being that knows you intimately just as it knows all other humans intimately and to, silently in your heart, give account of yourself is an important thought experiment we should all undertake. I personally cannot accept that somebody can live in a world where human beings lack food or medical care, suffer and die for this lack, and yet spend thousands of dollars on kidney surgery for an animal, would be able to look god in the face and not feel shame in their hearts. I am choosing not to look up the figures for the amounts of money spent on pet medical care, luxury foods, and insurance because I don't want to become depressed.

The End Times

Nobody profits from encouraging us to spend time and energy working through difficult human relationships that are ultimately fulfilling. The real end times scenario occurs when the advertising industry realises[4] that single humans living by themselves or in unattached groups (like share-houses) are much more profitable than family units and specifically people with children. If you consider babies, most advertising is for non-satanic products like clothing, nappies, etc (with some horrific exceptions). By and large, parenthood creates a vast array of unprofitable or low-margin expenses like education, healthcare, and unpaid domestic labour. Because parents are already often financially stressed, it is harder for parasitic psychic-terrorist companies to insert themselves to change behaviour in high margin ways. For example, Uber might be able to induce stressed parents to bulk-order groceries and have them delivered, but that is clearly less profitable than inducing a cultural change of millinials without children regularly ordering UberEats. A vicious circle takes place where high-energy rapid-response finger-on-the-pulse marketing strategies are actively opposed to human reproduction, preferring to keep people in an endless state of high disposable income, no capital/assets, and pure consumption. After all, human babies and networks of economic enmeshment encourage spending on low-margin staples, so it is natural for economic 'disruptors' to subtly and secretly oppose these things. Marx believed that wages tended towards the cost of reproduction, i.e. the minimal cost required to produce more workers. But in a competitive marketplace, why should such long-range thinking prevail? If we switch lenses from production to consumption, and specifically disrupted/new consumption, clearly the locally superior strategy is to siphon resources from the host before it becomes locked in to an array of time and money 'expenses'. In this way, the machine of contemporary capitalism adopts a well-attested strategy of other parasites such as blood flukes that destroy the reproductive capacity of their hosts.

Notes and Explanations

[1] A satanic arrangement refers to a 'deal with the devil', where some institution or arrangement appears to be satisfying a need, but its satisfaction of the need is tied to causing greater harm relating to embeddedness, control, or ownership. We need to remember that Satan doesn't offer Faust nothing: In all satanic arrangements such as social media addiction, Satan (in this case Facebook) offers to provide real social access, but through its resulting embeddedness it steals much more from us than it gives.

[2] It is important to emphasise the substitution of romantic partners or nuclear family members (including children) because in contemporary Western society these are the only relationships (other than those of employment) that confer obligation

[3] This is still the case in the country. In regional Australia you can still say "What's the dog worth to you?" and expect an answer that includes the sentimental value of the dog. In the city, you would just get indignant looks: How dare you put a price on my family member.

[4] I don't want to fall into the trap of saying that capitalism "desires" something or has some kind of overall telos. I refer instead to the process of random-mutation-followed-by-natual-selection by which some kind of trend developes and mestastasizes through the 'system'
