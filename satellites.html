The sky is restless now

The mad proliferation of satellites means that even without light pollution, the night sky is restless and temporary

Return Home

The word "planet" meant "wanderer"

It is hard to imagine, but there was a time where the planets were considered as objects that moved against the fixed backdrop of the stars. Nowadays, satellites crawl across the sky. Some time in the future, literary references to a peaceful sky will be confusing and require footnotes to explain to readers. Instead of humans in motion across a stationary world under an eternal sky, humans are static while the world around us is in ceaseless, restless motion.

What will be the impact of living under a twisting, shifting sky on human culture and our experience as creatures? Perhaps some guidance can be found from Duane Hamacher, who writes about the impact of a sky brightened by light pollution on Indigenous culture.
